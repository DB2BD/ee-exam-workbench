# Wait What Skill (by Matt Pocock)

Translates complex, opaque jargon and verbose theoretical derivations into **dead-simple, intuitive mental models**.

## 🎯 Protocol

1. **ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5)**: Use everyday analogies (e.g., water pipes for circuits, spinning tops for synchronous machines, gears for transformers).
2. **Bottom Line Up Front**: Start with the single most important takeaway in 1 sentence.
3. **Cut the Fluff**: Remove academic posturing; focus strictly on physical intuition and what you actually need to calculate on the exam.
